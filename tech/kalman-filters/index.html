The need for Kalman filters

Every sensor on a car is a little wrong. First, drive using only dead reckoning. On your next run, GPS updates snap the car to each noisy fix; after that, a Kalman filter combines GPS with speed and turn-rate sensors. When time runs out, the full true path and the route shown by the localization system appear together.
