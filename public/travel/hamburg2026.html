Hamburg 2026

On a work trip to Hamburg, Sophie and I made the most of our time there!

21st February 2026
We woke up early to get a head start into exploring the city. We visited the Miniatur Wunderland where they had models of almost everything under the sun. They even had the Monacco F1 race track modelled with cars zooming around. Afterwardsd we went to the old town to see the canals and the old buildings with winches to deliver goods. We saw the Elbphilharmonie as well before calling it a day.
22nd February 2026
We treated ourselves to a spa day and relaxed at the hotel. We enjoyed the pool, spa and even the sauna before hopping back on our flight into London.
