Bern 2026

On our way to meet up with Bettina and Dad in Switzerland, we needed to satiate our summer desires!

23rd May 2026
What an eventful day! The night before we got in late to Zurich airport and the got up early to get the train into Bern. We browsed the beautiful city and found the greenery enticing. We loved how cities in Switzerland had the rivers flowing through them. We had already been to Bern once in 2023 but this time in the summer it was even more spectacular. We swam in the lake which locals float down to get home from work. It was ice cold but so refreshing! We then randomly decided to go into the music museum and try out some of the instruments. We tried the theremin and even an alpenhorn! We both had a lot of trouble playing the horns though! In the evening was the main event, the bouldering competition where our very own Australian OCeania Mackenzie was competing. The atmosphere was very cool for our first bouldering comp and Oce won her first gold medal!!!!!
