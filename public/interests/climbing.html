Climbing

Our climbing jounrey first started some years back, around 2021. Our first experience was indoor bouldering in Sydney, Australia. Since then we have branched out into top roping, outdoor bouldering and even trad climbing! There has been ups and downs, but through it all we have had a lot of fun. You can read more about some of our adventures as they get updated here.

15th March 2025
We started out in the bouldering gyms with our high socks and rental shoes. We frequented NOMAD before we settled on 9 degrees aas our go to bouldering branch. We didn't know at this point just how obsessed we would become with climbing...
15th March 2025
Our first outdoor trip was not long after we started indoor climbing and so we were shocked when we were brought to Malacia Point in the Blue Mountains. Woefully unaware and unprepared for the sheer exposure of outdoor climbing in the Blue Mountains we geared up and abseiled off this beautiful route.
15th March 2025
15th March 2025
Our biggest adventure to date was trad climbing Cornerstone Rib in the warambungles. Almost 300m of vertical climbing on an exposed rock face was awesome but terrifying at the same time. It wasn't without its nervy moments though.Another pair of climbers whizzing past us sent a tennis ball sized rock tumbling less than 5m away from me while climbing and perching side by side with another person on a ledge barely big enough for 1 person. But the feeling of reaching the top after a full day of climbing was so rewarding I would do it again in a heartbeat.
